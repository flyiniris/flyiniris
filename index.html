Questions Couples Ask

The quick answers.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Milwaukee?

Wedding film collections vary widely with coverage hours, team, and film length, so we build every package around your actual day instead of a fixed menu. Share your date and a few details and we'll send exact pricing plus your date's availability right away.

Do you travel outside Milwaukee?

Yes. Travel within 120 miles of Milwaukee is free, and we film weddings across Wisconsin: Madison, Green Bay, Door County, Lake Geneva, and beyond. Farther out, we add a small per-mile travel charge.

How long until we get our wedding film?

Your teaser lands within a week of the wedding. The full film is typically delivered within 12 to 16 weeks, streaming Netflix-style so you can relive it anytime, on any device.

Do we get the whole day or just a highlight film?

Both. Every package includes your cinematic highlight film plus the FOMO Documentary Edit: the raw, uncut film of your entire day. Every toast, every dance, everything you missed while you were getting married.

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you have your date and venue. We cap our calendar each year on purpose, and popular Saturdays go first. Check your date with the form above for a real-time answer.
