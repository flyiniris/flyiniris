Filming at Green Bay Country Club

Green Bay Country Club is the venue behind the film we chose to feature on our homepage, and that choice was not an accident. Taylor and Kane's "Main Characters" film came out of a day where the venue kept handing us gorgeous frames: manicured golf course grounds stretching out behind every portrait, mature trees filtering the afternoon sun, and a classic ballroom that glows once the reception begins.

The grounds are the star here. In summer, the fairways turn gold in the last hour before sunset, and we build the portrait timeline around that window whenever the schedule allows. A short golf cart ride out onto the course opens up sweeping backdrops that feel a world away from the reception, and it costs almost nothing in timeline minutes. Back inside, the club's warm, traditional architecture gives us a rich setting for toasts, dances, and the quieter in-between moments.

Country club weddings reward a filmmaker who plans the day around light, and that is how Sierra & Sean approach every wedding here: outdoors while the sun is generous, indoors when the room turns golden, and always close enough to catch the real moments without intruding on them.

Planning your wedding at Green Bay Country Club

Getting ready

Wherever you get ready in Green Bay, we plan travel so we arrive with plenty of margin to film details, letters, and those last calm minutes before everything begins.

Ceremony to reception flow

The club makes the transition easy for guests, which makes it easy on film. While cocktail hour carries the energy, we take you out onto the course for golden hour portraits, then slip back inside before introductions so the film never misses a beat of the party.

Our favorite moment

Golden hour on the fairways. In Taylor and Kane's film, that stretch of warm light on the open course gave us the most cinematic frames of the whole day. If you pick this venue, protect fifteen minutes for it. You will be glad you did.
