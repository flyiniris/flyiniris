Parties

This Agreement is entered into between Flyin' Iris Media Productions LLC ("Videographer," owners Sierra Hernitz & Sean Adamec) and the Client(s) named in this signed agreement.

Event Details

The work.

Here's what we deliver and when. Final artistic call is ours, but we'll do everything we can to capture what matters to you.

Deliverables

All films delivered in 4K resolution (highlight film) and 1080p (FOMO documentary, if included). Drone footage included when conditions and regulations permit. Sierra Hernitz is FAA Part 107 certified.

Delivery Timeline

Teaser film: Within 2 weeks of event date.

Highlight film: Within 12 weeks of event date.

Ceremony edit: Within 16 weeks of event date.

FOMO Documentary Edit (if included): Within 16 weeks of event date.

Access period: All films remain on your private viewing page indefinitely. We recommend downloading and backing up all files within 90 days of delivery.

Services

Flyin' Iris Media Productions LLC agrees to provide videographers to perform video services for Client at the Event in a professional manner according to prevailing industry standards. Videographer may employ assistants as needed. Videographer is not responsible for the impact on the final product of any interference caused by guests or other vendors at the Event. Videographer will make every effort to obtain, but cannot guarantee delivery of, any specific footage requested by the Client due to lack of control over the Event, its participants, weather, and venue rules or restrictions.

Meals

Client shall arrange with the caterer to provide meal(s) for all videographers onsite at the Event.

Exclusivity

Client agrees that Videographer is hired exclusive of any other videography service provider. No other videographers, paid or unpaid, are permitted to provide the same or similar services at the Event without prior written consent from Videographer.

The money.

This agreement is binding upon signature AND retainer clearing within 30 days of signature. If the retainer is not received within 30 days, this agreement automatically expires and your date is released back to general availability.

Payment Terms

Payments accepted via Credit/Debit Card or ACH Bank Transfer (via Stripe). The first payment (the retainer) is due immediately upon signing this agreement. Subsequent payments follow the schedule selected above.

If Client fails to remit any scheduled payment by the due date, Videographer reserves the right to terminate this agreement with no obligation to attend the event or refund prior payments. Recurring payments on a multi-payment plan are charged automatically to the payment method on file.

Non-Refundable

All payments under this agreement are non-refundable. The retainer secures Client's wedding date and removes that date from general availability.

If life happens.

Cancellation, rescheduling, weather, and force majeure. The boring stuff that matters when something goes sideways.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

If Client cancels, Client shall provide notice as soon as possible. Videographer has no obligation to refund any monies previously paid. Client is not relieved of payment obligations for cancelled or rescheduled services unless Parties agree in writing. If Videographer secures another client for the original date, Videographer may, at sole discretion, excuse all or a portion of Client's outstanding balance.

Force Majeure

Either party may be excused from performance in the event of natural disaster, war, terrorism, or other events outside either party's control. Videographer will immediately notify Client and issue a pro-rated refund for undelivered Services.

Weather

In the event of undesirable weather for outdoor filming, Client is responsible for having an indoor or alternative location. Due to potential equipment damage, Videographer may move to an alternative indoor location at their discretion. No refunds for weather-related limitations. Drone Services: Weather and location restrictions apply. Videographer will not fly if FAA regulations restrict the flight.
