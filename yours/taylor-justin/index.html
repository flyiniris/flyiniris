Your actual question

Team or S&S

Here is the honest answer, and it is simpler than most people expect.

The two packages deliver the exact same thing. Same edit style, same highlight film lengths, same drone coverage, same audio, same Netflix-style delivery page, same everything. The only difference is who is holding the camera.

Team is one of our videographers filming your day. They shoot our style, Sierra edits the film, and the work goes out under our name. It starts at $2,800.

S&S Experience is the two of us, Sierra and Sean, filming your day personally as a two-person team. Two cameras moving at once means more angles, more of the small stuff, and GoPro coverage we can only run with a second shooter. It starts at $3,800.
